The agreed gameplay direction is an archaeological site at the foot of the
solid monument, with an original cosmetic armor-trim template and a map to the **unique large
Colossus**. Maps initially share this destination; smaller Colossi may repeat
but are not selected map targets. Archaeological finds are shared and finite;
maps may be copied/shared and later visitors may seek another Obelisk. Detailed
layout, exact trim artwork/application, reward implementation and numeric
rarity values remain open; this gameplay is not implemented yet.

The approved trim motif follows the Obelisk's angular geometric forms. Copying
uses the vanilla principle: one template + seven diamonds + one copper block
yields two templates. Copper is a material link to the Colossi, not a stat bonus.
