# Titan Skull gameplay: inhabited remains

Recorded: October 1, 2026. Updated: October 8, 2026. These are approved gameplay
directions; no authored encounter system is selected. Alpha 4.9 selects crowned Skull V4 in
four palettes and Skull 2 V2 embedded-v1. This page applies to both skull designs;
their existing interiors and current terrain-placement accessibility are accepted
as-is for now. Mob spawning remains unchanged and natural; no loot chests are selected.

## Exploration and mobs

- The decaying skull and its cavities may be inhabited by **ordinary Minecraft
  mobs through unchanged natural spawning**. The user explicitly selected leaving
  this alone: **no additional spawners, placed enemies, guaranteed roster/counts,
  spawn-rate overrides or custom persistence logic** at either skull design.
  Skeletons/spiders are examples of possible natural inhabitants, not promised
  encounters or an outstanding authored mob-selection decision.
- No custom boss, special Titan enemy, or mandatory trial/key system is planned.
- No exclusive reward item is required. The earlier Bone Resonator and trophy
  suggestions are **not selected for this design**.
- **No loot chests at either skull design.** Exploration, atmosphere, ordinary
  natural encounters and normal block/mob drops carry the site; no chest reward
  or replacement scripted reward is required.
- **October 8 approval:** the existing interiors of **both skull designs** already
  fit the desired exploration. Keep their current cavities rather than authoring
  new rooms or imposing a mouth-to-eye-socket circuit. **User clarification:**
  current accessibility at terrain placements also fits; leave it unchanged for
  now. No additional access/terrain-clearing review or edits are in the current
  scope. This records acceptance of the current state, not a universal technical
  guarantee for every possible terrain placement.

## Frequency and future variants

**Updated hierarchy:** **Obelisks and both skull designs share the relatively
most frequent tier**, followed by rarer **Tower, small Colossi and World Tree**,
then the **unique large Colossus**. All tiers must still feel **genuinely rare and
widely separated**, not repetitive background scenery. The approved starting
candidate ratios below are not guaranteed successful-site ratios; they supersede
the older requirement that Obelisks be clearly rarer than skulls. Validate actual
distances/density in real worlds against the accepted schematic scale.

Treat this as a combined family budget, not a full independent spawn frequency
for every added variant. The world should retain open space between discoveries.
Further bone-site designs remain future work. Crowned-skull palettes currently
share a set, while Skull 2 retains an independent provisional rate; the approved
combined family budget is not fully implemented yet.

The user approved **30% combined skull chance among shared relic opportunities
within broad Obelisk regions**, and a much lower chance elsewhere. The user approved
a **6,000-block region radius** and a **2,000-block minimum horizontal cross-relic
center-to-center distance** and **5% combined skull chance outside**. The user also
approved equal design shares: **15% each inside / 2.5% each outside**. Crowned-skull
palettes share that design's portion. Obelisk attempts have 30% candidate chance;
Tower/small Colossus/Tree have 2% each, leaving 34% empty inside / 59% outside.
The user approved
**at most one shared repeatable-relic
opportunity per approximately 5,000 × 5,000-block area**; failed/empty attempts
remain empty and the unique large site has a separate selection. This is
not a per-chunk chance, extra skull roll or guaranteed skull beside every Obelisk.
Natural Obelisk regions **must neither overlap nor touch**: the user selected
**at least 15,000 blocks between their centers**, leaving 3,000 blocks between
edges with the approved equal 6,000-block radius. This replaces the generic
2,000-block floor for this pair; changing radii must still preserve disjoint,
non-touching regions. Rejected Obelisks cannot anchor regions.
The conditional budget and deterministic anchor planning are not implemented.

A [second independent skull reference](../docs/TITAN_SKULL_2_CONCEPT.md) has been
selected from the opening of Instagram reel `Db9KCtWi0eG`. Its independent
[V1 model/export](../docs/TITAN_SKULL_2_V1.md) is historical; its V2 successor is
integrated and visually accepted in Alpha 4.9. It is not another V4 ground palette.
It follows this family's ordinary-mob,
no-special-item gameplay direction.

Numeric planning rates are selected; current alpha worldgen settings are unchanged.

## Lore direction

The Titans' exact nature and the campaign's motive are **lost to time**. The
known internal history is that they **worshipped the World Tree and received
their strength and size from it**. The central large Colossus and its smaller,
crewed companions conducted the Titan-clearing campaign thousands of years ago.
**No living Titans remain today.** Both skull designs and bones are ancient remains,
now inhabited or reused by ordinary mobs and players and reclaimed by nature.
Their present inhabitants do not imply the Titans' return, a revival quest or boss.

The lore review calls for visible links between these remains and the machinery,
not only a textual connection. A machine fragment embedded in bone, corresponding
damage on the Colossus, or later expedition marks are candidate clues only.
Select a restrained shared visual vocabulary during future design; none of these
details is approved anatomy or a required addition to every skull. This does not
authorize changes to accepted V4 exports or introduce living Titans/custom bosses.

Obelisks **marked cleared territories**. Titan skulls should be scattered across
their broad surroundings; the [distribution contract](../../../docs/technical/RELIC_DISTRIBUTION.md)
specifies distances/budget/valid anchors. Runtime biome/terrain and realized-density
validation remains. Do not interpret this as a skull guaranteed next
to every monument. Skull 2's sword owner and exact death circumstances remain unknown.
