# Titan Skull gameplay: inhabited remains

Recorded: October 1, 2026. Updated: October 2, 2026. These are approved gameplay
directions, not implemented encounters. V3 is selected with natural worldgen in
Alpha 3; the accepted V4 studies await integration.

## Exploration and mobs

- The decaying skull and its cavities are inhabited by **ordinary Minecraft
  mobs**. A few local encounters reinforce the feeling of overgrown remains.
- Skeletons and spiders are suitable examples, not a finalized spawn roster.
  Exact mob types, counts, spawn method, and persistence remain open.
- No custom boss, special Titan enemy, or mandatory trial/key system is planned.
- No exclusive reward item is required. The earlier Bone Resonator and trophy
  suggestions are **not selected for this design**.
- Ordinary loot may be added later, but no specific chest or guaranteed reward
  is decided. Exploration, atmosphere, and ordinary encounters can carry the site.
- The detailed interior and exploration route will be designed later; do not
  assume the current omitted-air export already supplies an in-terrain route.

## Frequency and future variants

**Titan skulls and other skull/bone variants are intended to be the most common
relic family**, followed by clearly rarer Obelisks. The large Colossus is intended
to be **unique per game world**; planned smaller Colossi may occur multiple times,
with their exact rarity still open. These rules are not yet final worldgen balance.

Treat this as a combined family budget, not a full independent spawn frequency
for every added variant. The world should retain open space between discoveries.
Additional skull and bone variants are future designs, not already implemented
assets. Their forms, sizes, and placement rules remain open.

A [second independent skull reference](../docs/TITAN_SKULL_2_CONCEPT.md) has been
selected from the opening of Instagram reel `Db9KCtWi0eG`. Its independent
[V1 model/export](../docs/TITAN_SKULL_2_V1.md) now awaits visual review;
it is not another V4 ground palette. It follows this family's ordinary-mob,
no-special-item gameplay direction.

No numeric rates or current alpha worldgen settings have been changed.

## Lore direction

In the working history, a Colossus was sent to the planet to kill the Titans.
The skulls and bones are their surviving remains, now occupied by ordinary mobs
and reclaimed by nature. The mobs are current inhabitants, not proof that the
Titans themselves have returned.

The lore review calls for visible links between these remains and the machinery,
not only a textual connection. A machine fragment embedded in bone, corresponding
damage on the Colossus, or later expedition marks are candidate clues only.
Select a restrained shared visual vocabulary during future design; none of these
details is approved anatomy or a required addition to every skull. This does not
authorize changes to accepted V4 exports or introduce living Titans/custom bosses.

Obelisks may have been wayfinders or markers of places where Titans had already
been destroyed. The sender, motivation, and exact history remain open.

Shared lore and frequency hierarchy:
[lore bible](../../../docs/project/LORE-BIBLE.md#relative-discovery-hierarchy).

## Open decisions

- Mob roster, density, and whether encounters use natural spawning or authored
  placement; an endlessly active mob spawner is not implied.
- Detailed routes and terrain clearing inside the skull.
- Any ordinary loot and container placement.
- New skull/bone designs and the shared family generation budget.
- Visible links to the Titan conflict, coordinated with the Colossus and Obelisk
  rather than invented independently for each site; see the
  [lore/gameplay review](../../../docs/project/LORE_GAMEPLAY_REVIEW.md).
