## Historical planning record

**Skull 2's current Alpha 4.9 appearance and terrain integration were visually
accepted by the user on October 7, 2026.** Three new Skull screenshots accompany
the [acceptance record](../../docs/project/reviews/alpha4-9-user-acceptance-20261007.json).
This accepts the existing shallow-footprint/bounded-contact revision, not new
changes to the crowned Skull variants or encounter gameplay. The native-leaf
cavity observation remains a separate technical finding.

**Alpha 4.9 keeps all raw Skull exports unchanged.** Skull 2's natural template
alone omits the deeply buried slice below raw Y22, with ground Y28 unchanged.
Its bounded processor uses adjoining art material for complete contacts up to
two cells within the six-block surface band; longer caves remain open rather
than receiving dirt walls. Rock Skull is unchanged. See
[the cave candidates](../../docs/testing/CAVE_CONTACTS_20261007.md) and
[current runtime evidence](../../docs/testing/ALPHA_4_9_TEST_REPORT.md).

**Alpha 4.8 selects rock Skull and Skull 2 embedded-v1**, with no authored ground
platforms and six blocks deeper burial. Every anatomical cell and the rigid
sword are preserved; the other three crowned-Skull palettes remain ground-v3.
See [the candidates](../../docs/testing/ROOTED_EMBEDDING_20261007.md) and
[current runtime status](../../docs/testing/ALPHA_4_8_TEST_REPORT.md). The preserved
Alpha 4.7 native-leaf cavity observation is not relabeled as passing.

Historical candidates: [crowned Skull ground-v3 and Skull 2 V2](../../docs/project/EMBEDDING_20261006.md)
lower the central deposits and settle the remains into the terrain. New art and
placement contracts are selected together in Alpha 4.6.

**Alpha 4.6 selects V4's four ground-v3 palettes and Skull 2 V2**: crowned-Skull

settled skull/sword pose. See [embedding artwork](../../docs/project/EMBEDDING_20261006.md)
and [runtime status](../../docs/testing/ALPHA_4_6_TEST_REPORT.md). These changed
exports remain visual candidates; historical Alpha 4 art acceptance is preserved.

The agreed gameplay direction leaves existing interiors/access and natural mob
spawning unchanged, with **no loot chests or authored encounters** at either skull.
Obelisks and both skull designs share the relatively most frequent tier, followed
by rarer Tower/small Colossi/Tree, then the unique large Colossus. **Every relic is
still genuinely rare**, with approved starting rates/distances in the gameplay
plan and [distribution contract](../../docs/technical/RELIC_DISTRIBUTION.md).
Actual density needs runtime validation. Alpha 4.9 does not enforce uniqueness or the shared budget.

The second skull is a separate design, not another revision or ground
palette of the current crowned skull. Together with later bone variants, it
should make multiple fallen Titans legible in the landscape without requiring
living Titans or new special rewards.
