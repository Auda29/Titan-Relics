## Observed in the reference

[Josh White, reel DbqXYoVx2hR](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbqXYoVx2hR/),
0–28 seconds inspected at two-second intervals.

- An extremely dark, angular vertical structure contrasts with a pale open plain.
- Long parallel vertical strips, recessed lines and deep openings govern the
  facade. The outline is damaged and stepped, rather than a clean rectangle.
- One conspicuous rounded opening interrupts the otherwise angular structure;
  other openings are narrow vertical slots or irregular missing wall sections.
- The foot flares into damaged supporting material; small posts/buildings near
  it strengthen scale. Other large shapes appear behind it in haze.
- The large horizontal object on the right lifts and leaves the scene. It is
  an aircraft, **not** part of the planned ruin footprint.
- The top remains outside the frame. Neither its original roof nor full height
  is observable. Foreground people cannot directly calibrate the distant tower.

## Destruction and stable silhouette

**Heavy structural ruin:** target **30–35% missing reconstructed exterior wall
area**, evaluated across the four elevations of an imagined intact shell.
This is an authoring budget, not a measurement of the video.

- One large vertical facade loss and one smaller side breach, rather than many
  equally spaced holes.
- Three unequal upper terminations at roughly **156 / 168 / 180** above ground;
  this newly designed crown is explicitly not visible in the reference.
- Retain at least two broad ground-to-top load paths. Broken cross-connections
  may expose sky, but no unsupported hanging upper tower segments.
- Debris concentrates below actual missing sections. Keep the approach mostly
  open; avoid a circular rubble pedestal or complete manufactured pale plain.
- Reserve a lower hall volume around **18 W × 22 H × 20 D** and a surviving
  intermediate ledge at **64–80** above ground. These are architectural
  allowances; stairs, routes and interior construction belong to V5.

## Ground and material direction

Use a dry open basin/desert-edge setting. The bottom 12 blocks are a foundation
allowance, not permission to bury the entrance. Keep side elevations legible and
check placement height before worldgen. Dark deepslate/basalt-like structural
mass, restrained warm weathering and sparse dull metal accents suit the scene;
an exact Minecraft palette and resource budget are still to be tested.

## What is authored rather than observed

The complete top, rear facade, 48-block depth, room allowances, rubble positions
and absolute dimensions are design choices. No city, aircraft, permanent fog or
repeated skyscraper floor grid is required to establish the reference identity.
First model review should compare front/side silhouette, the dominant negative
spaces, and ground-to-top continuity before small surface detail.
