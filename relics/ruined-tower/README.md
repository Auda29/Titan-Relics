## Historical planning record

**Lore approved October 8:** the unfinished base from which the ancient Titan-
clearing campaign was organized. Never-completed construction accounts for its
stronger dilapidation relative to the Colossi. Current mobs and players may reuse
the site, thousands of years later. Incomplete player-built ascent, five ordinary
one-time Endermen and finite 5% eye/otherwise 4–6 pearls are specified in
[gameplay](gameplay/README.md); actual builder layout/runtime validation remain.
No model changes follow from this lore.

**V7 ground-v3 is the latest visual candidate:** connected collapsed side bays and a broken
corner, retaining 220 visible layers. See the [Alpha 4.5 follow-up](../../docs/project/EMBEDDING_20261006.md).

V5 introduced broken side elevations, partial masonry
courses and a low meadow-soil foot, retaining the user's 220 visible layers. See
the [October 6 follow-up](../../docs/project/GROUND_WEATHERING_20261006.md).
Alpha 4.9 retains V7 ground-v3 and its tighter slope envelope; see the
[current runtime status](../../docs/testing/ALPHA_4_9_TEST_REPORT.md).
Alpha 4.5's V6 and Alpha 4.4's V5 evidence remain historical;
client visual acceptance is still pending.
