## Reference hierarchy and observed identity

1. Primary: [archived first concept](../archive/first-concept/README.md), especially
   its [source and NBT review](../archive/first-concept/generated/colossus-review.png).
   Existing export: **99 × 66 × 71**, **26,169 blocks**.
2. Secondary: [original Colossus reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaoBxUEiBNa/),
   locally preserved stills `colossus-01.png` and `colossus-04.png`, for fallen
   machine mass, weathering and the detached-limb relationship.

The archive already establishes a distinct machine: a **beveled angular helmet
with a narrow dark visor**, a compact rounded chest with a large circular breach,
unequal shoulders, a grounded left arm and a separate right forearm/hand. Its
palette is predominantly cool dark stone-like material with small patina accents.

The large accepted Colossus's round diving helmet, long forearm and current
copper treatment are not a reason to overwrite this smaller design's identity.
The archived source dimensions are reliable; the distant reel's human figures
are not a ruler for a second robot.

## Working dimensions

| Element | Proposed dimensions / placement |
| --- | --- |
| Complete local envelope | **99 W × 76 H × 88 D** |
| Visible body height | **66**, plus **10** for buried lower completion |
| Body including shoulders | Approximately **76** wide |
| Cuirass | Approximately **46 W × 46 H × 34 D**, following the archive |
| Helmet | Approximately **24 W × 20 H × 22 D** |
| Helmet/shoulder width ratio | **24/76 ≈0.32** |
| Circular chest rupture | **22–24** diameter, irregular broken rim |
| Detached forearm + hand group | Approximately **38–44** long, **16–20** wide |
| Low-neck exposure | **5–7** high, visibly connected to the torso |

Keep the original width and above-ground scale. The added vertical envelope is
for burial and a plausible torn lower-body continuation; the extra depth allows
an irregular ground join and a few debris pieces, not a larger main body. This is
a proposed new asset envelope, **not** a claim that the archived 99 × 66 × 71 NBT
already has these bounds or needs a uniform rescale.

At 66 visible blocks it is approximately half the accepted large Colossus's
visible height. A two-block player is about 1/33 of that height, so the repeated
site is still monumental without competing with the unique large landmark.

## Pose and destruction

**Moderate battle damage and weathering:** target **20–25% missing reconstructed
outer-armour area**. A detached but still present forearm does not itself count
as removed material. The archive's existing ruptures count toward the target;
do not add another full damage budget on top.

- Keep the compact seated/slumped pose and low connected head.
- Preserve the primary circular chest break, one torn shoulder socket and the
  single detached **anatomically right** forearm.
- Lay that forearm against the ground. For the revised hand, use a low wrist,
  palm-up orientation and mildly curled fingers; preserve its small compact
  scale rather than importing the large hand wholesale.
- Retain the grounded left arm/knuckles as a convincing support.
- Add at most one secondary back/side breach, roughly **8–12** across. Show
  internal supporting members through openings without a second chest motif.
- Put burial around the lower torso, elbow and palm heel. Keep the visor, chest
  outline and some curled fingertips readable from the primary approach.
- Limit scattered fragments to **3–5** pieces near their source fractures.

## Material and later gameplay boundary

Prefer the archive's dark gray structural identity, warm dirt/moss joins and
restrained copper accents. Avoid a new mass of raw iron blocks; exact salvage
counts require a later block export and separate balancing. The large Colossus's
25,423-copper/90-raw-iron inventory is not automatically copied into this repeated
design. Any retained salvage must leave neck and load paths visually coherent.

Reserve the existing chest cavity for later access review; no Core, Magnet,
vault or trial reward is inherited automatically. This is the planned repeatable
small-Colossus family, with occurrence and gameplay still separate work.

Hidden rear details, lower completion and revised hand anatomy are authored
choices. The archived Blender/NBT files remain preserved; later modeling must
start in a new source/configuration. First review should compare archive and new
silhouette at identical scale, including the detached limb and ground datum.
