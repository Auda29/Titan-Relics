# Alpha 4.5 screenshot follow-up — settle the relics into the ground

The candidates are now selected, with their paired burial/slope contracts, for
[Alpha 4.6 integration](../testing/ALPHA_4_6_TEST_REPORT.md). This art report's
offline checks remain separate from that build's fresh Minecraft validation.

The user agreed with all four findings in the 30-image Alpha 4.5 review:
remaining soil/rock pedestals, Skull 2's suspended posterior, geometric Tree moss
pads, and the Tower's broad closed side wall. The follow-up changes central
deposits and nominal burial together, rather than repeating edge-only erosion.

## Saved-site diagnosis

`tools/audit_alpha45_embedding.py` reads the saved natural run
`build/smoke/run-20261006-104446-781ff9011d44/`. Its report hash and exact source
hashes are recorded under
`build/screenshot-review/20261006-alpha4-5/embedding-audit/`.
Vertical sections separate authored soil, additional protected support, art and
surviving unmasked terrain. They are post-placement measurements, not a recovery
of the native surface overwritten by the structure.

- Crowned Skull soil still reaches **25 blocks above nominal ground**; the dry
  and rock exports each contain 54,769 above-/at-ground soil cells.
- Small Colossus soil reaches +7. At its saved hillside site, the derived toe is
  exposed by the drop toward the water-filled depression behind the body.
- Skull 2 has substantial open space beneath the rear cranium, despite the soil
  foundation passing technical contact tests.
- The Tree's soil only reaches +1; the conspicuous raised pads are separate
  `Root contact moss` meshes inherited from V2, previously protected as art.

## New candidates

### Crowned Skull, four palettes — ground-v3

The complete anatomy stays identical in NBT coordinates. Nominal ground moves
from Y8 to Y16: **eight blocks deeper burial**. Central soil is cut to at most
ground +1, with coherent uneven caps. High soil underneath elevated skull arches
no longer locks the entire mound. Low bone contacts remain supported.

### Small Colossus V7 — ground-v3

Nominal ground moves from Y9 to Y13: **four blocks deeper burial**. The torso,
hand, armour and growth are exact source-block matches. Central earth is reduced
to the same low surface band, so it does not form a high support cushion behind
the body.

### World Tree V4.1 — ground-v3

Nominal ground and the accepted wood/foliage stay unchanged. The low moss pads
are explicitly included in this ground revision: lowered into broken thin moss
patches over earth, with their regular tiers removed. Source terrain and low moss
are mutable; roots, trunk, canopy and worked stones remain protected.
The builder compares all wood and leaf cells against accepted V4 directly.

### Titan Skull 2 V2

The skull, its attached growth and embedded sword rotate as one assembly by
16 degrees around the lower front, then settle three source units downward.
This lowers the rear into the ground without raising a new mound beneath it.
Deposits are replaced by low grass/rooted-earth patches. The face apertures,
sword sections and bone-to-sword contacts are validated after voxelization.
The export scale is kept close to V1; the lower total height comes from the pose.

### Ruined Tower V7

Two continuous collapsed side bays join the previous isolated openings.
One loss reaches through a corner to interrupt the rectangular silhouette.
Shell, overlaid courses and buttresses are cut together; storeys and stairs are
exposed. The Tower retains **220 visible layers** and the prior lower-annex work.
Its final `ground-v3` derivative also lowers the soil foot, so the newer Blender
source does not reintroduce the older broad ground apron.

## Placement and integration contract

The [candidate index](embedding-candidates-20261006.json) is generated by
`tools/index_embedding_candidates.py`.
The deeper raw-template ground values **must** be used during integration, along
with the narrowed slope envelopes: 4 blocks for meadow/dry/sand Skulls, Small
Colossus and Skull 2; 6 for rock Skull, Tree and Tower. The previous slope
allowance was 12 for these Skulls, Small Colossus and Tree, and 10 for Tower.
These are candidate placement settings, not new measured runtime acceptance.

This reduces exposure of the support skirt at admitted sites. The current
all-grid placement checks still sample the site; they do not guarantee that
every intervening ravine is detected. A fresh natural run is required to assess
actual placements and locator performance with the tighter envelopes.


The ground-v3 exports are explicit block-space derivatives. Their dedicated
configs use `authoring: block-embedding-derivative`; they are not ObjToSchematic
inputs. All preserved sources are checksum-verified. Builder validation covers
immutable art, the soil-height cap, low art supports inside template bounds,
schematic/NBT equality, bounds and lossless tile reconstruction.

Deeply buried Y0 undersides can lie outside the new finite template after the
slope/padding change. They are reported separately for runtime substrate checks;
no out-of-bounds cells are invented and template checks do not certify those
native contacts. Removing elevated soil beneath skull arches is intentional,
so ground-v2's blanket contact-column-identity rule does not apply to ground-v3.
