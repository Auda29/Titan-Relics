# Titan Relics 1.0.0-beta.5

*Colossal ruins of a forgotten age*

Beta 5 relaxes the Minecraft 1.21.1 Lithostitched dependency pin to the `1.8.x`
patch line so CurseForge App and Modrinth installs work out of the box with
Lithostitched 1.8.1, and ships a full gameplay walkthrough & item reference.
It is a public Beta, selected as GitHub Latest. Full Survival and long-session
acceptance remain outstanding.

## Changes since Beta 4

- **Minecraft 1.21.1 Lithostitched `1.8.x` support:** Fabric 1.21.1 now accepts
  `>=1.8.0 <1.9.0` and NeoForge 1.21.1 accepts `[1.8.0,1.9.0)` instead of
  requiring `1.8.0` exclusively. Both Lithostitched `1.8.0` and `1.8.1` work
  without manual downgrades, and existing `1.8.0` worlds reload with identical
  planning fingerprints and unique Colossus destinations on `1.8.1`.
- **Full gameplay walkthrough & item reference:** added
  [WALKTHROUGH.md](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/blob/v1.0.0-beta.5/docs/players/WALKTHROUGH.md)
  and dedicated German/English guide pages on the website
  ([EN](https://auda29.github.io/Titan-Relics/guide-en.html) ·
  [DE](https://auda29.github.io/Titan-Relics/guide.html)), documenting Obelisk
  archaeology, the Great Colossus Trial & Vault, Survival-mode requirements when
  testing in Creative worlds, every exclusive item, crafting recipes, and all
  landmarks.
- **Keep strict pins on other targets:** Minecraft 26.2 (`1.8.0`), 1.21.11
  (`1.7.2`) and 26.3 (`2.0.6`) retain their exact verified Lithostitched pins.

## Existing worlds and setup

Back up saves and install exactly one matching JAR with its required dependencies.
Beta 5 preserves the Beta 4 generation revision (revision 3), v5 Colossus search,
surface-building overlap filter and V6 interiors. Existing worlds keep their
frozen rules and interior revision even in unexplored chunks. Do not downgrade
worlds to a version that cannot read their saved revisions.

## Validation and known limits

- **Lithostitched 1.8.0 / 1.8.1 (1.21.1 Fabric & NeoForge):** verified bytecode
  compatibility of `LithostitchedInternalHooks`, `DelegatingStructure` and
  `PlacementCondition`; ran isolated dedicated-server tests on both loaders
  confirming world creation on `1.8.0` → upgrade & reload on `1.8.1`, plus fresh
  world creation & restart on `1.8.1` with identical planning fingerprints and
  unique Colossus placement.
- **Minecraft 26.3 singleplayer client reload (Fabric & NeoForge):** verified
  isolated client world creation, clean shutdown, full client restart and saved
  world reload on both loaders with zero crashes or world-load rejections.
- Full release CI validates all eight targets before publishing player JARs.
  Platform moderation and public download availability are separate; confirmed
  platform file IDs and hashes are persisted in `platform-upload-state.json`.
  Full Survival, long-session and final menu visual acceptance remain outstanding.
