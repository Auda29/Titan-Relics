# Titan Relics 1.0.0-beta.4

*Colossal ruins of a forgotten age*

Beta 4 combines the current discovery, surface-building overlap and tower-reward
changes from PRs #2, #3 and #4. It is a public Beta, selected as GitHub Latest.
It does not claim complete Survival or long-session acceptance.

## Changes since Beta 3

- Finer Colossus search cells check additional nearby sites before expanding
  farther away. Discoveries retains its 4,000-block minimum. A suitable biome
  and terrain footprint are still required; this is not a maximum travel distance.
- A deterministic 50% decision suppresses a targeted surface building when its
  pieces collide with solid relic cells. The relic keeps its site and model.
  The other half of buildings can still overlap. Underground structures, small
  exempt ruins, unsupported structures and the protected initial-spawn area
  retain their existing behavior. Decisions persist across reloads.
- New ruined towers include a chest niche on an upper platform, framed with
  deepslate, basalt and a lantern. It guarantees one Feather Falling IV book,
  3–6 diamonds and 8–16 iron ingots. The lower Ender chest keeps its separate
  loot and one-time initialization. Neither chest refills after removal/reload.
- Short Sword Skull contacts now recognize a continuous, supported column of
  falling soil as ground. This fixes the reproduced Immersive Weathering case
  without replacing native soil, increasing the two-block fill limit, filling
  longer voids, or changing V1–V5 world behavior.
- The shared repository gallery includes eight additional unchanged originals:
  three Creative-test landscape shots and five author-approved tower views.
  Living World and shaders also contribute terrain, vegetation and lighting.

## Existing worlds and setup

Back up saves and install exactly one matching JAR with its required dependencies.
To test all new generation behavior, create a new world with structures enabled
and explicitly select a preset in Create World → World → Relic generation.
New worlds use generation revision 3, the v5 Colossus-search policy, the surface
building rule and V6 interiors. Existing worlds keep their frozen rules and
interior revision even in unexplored chunks. There is no retrogen and no new
upper chest is inserted into an old world's towers. Do not downgrade new worlds
to a version that cannot read their saved revisions.

## Validation and known limits

The isolated native surface-filter comparison preserves relic plans and sampled
model blocks, suppresses a selected colliding village, keeps another village,
and verifies restart, legacy-save and initial-spawn behavior. The Mansion target
uses its correct registered ID. User Creative tests used locate and teleport;
they establish selected placements rather than Survival discovery density.

The new tower niche is visually accepted by the author. A separate native
Fabric 26.2 test naturally generates its complete footprint, checks Feather
Falling IV and loot amounts, restarts without changing either chest, removes
both chests and restarts again without refilling them.

A new isolated world reproduces the supplied seed and placement. Before the
contact fix it has 42 short unsupported contacts with all 25,149 model blocks
intact. With the fix its strict check passes: zero model mismatches, zero short
unsupported contacts, complete generated chunks and full protection masks.
These counts compare the same site, unlike the earlier 43-contact edited save
and the historical 71-contact site. They do not establish universal placement
or Survival acceptance. Player-confirmed edits to wood, lava and moss are
excluded from generation-failure claims.

A V5 reload with the new binary retains its old contacts and all four Relics
journals byte-for-byte. The 57 historical resources and all 60 PR-4 template,
processor and gameplay-manifest files remain unchanged. See the
[contact-fix evidence](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/blob/v1.0.0-beta.4/docs/testing/SWORD_SKULL_CONTACT_FIX_20261111.md).

The contact correction first ships with V6 interiors in Beta 4. Released V1–V5
worlds retain their previous behavior, including in newly explored chunks.
Already placed structures are not repaired retrospectively. Unreleased V6
development worlds receive the corrected handling for newly generated pieces.

The full release workflow validates all eight targets before publishing player
JARs. Platform upload acceptance and public availability are separate; see the
release's platform-upload-state.json for confirmed file IDs and hashes.
Full Survival, long-session and final menu visual acceptance remain outstanding.

See the [latest playtest report](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/blob/v1.0.0-beta.4/docs/testing/USER_PLAYTEST_20261111_AFTERNOON.md)
and [tower test record](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/blob/v1.0.0-beta.4/docs/project/TOWER_UPPER_CHEST_20261111.md).
