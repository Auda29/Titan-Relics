# Titan Relics 1.0.0-beta.1

*Colossal ruins of a forgotten age*

First public beta. This is a testing release, not a stable release.
The author has approved publication before the full natural Survival playthrough.
That playthrough will use this beta, and feedback will inform later beta updates.
Survival balance, discovery pacing and long-session behavior remain under review.

## Explore and recover

- Find monumental Overworld ruins: the large and small Colossus, titan skulls,
  weathered obelisks, a World Tree and a ruined tower.
- Investigate archaeology, recover expedition maps and explore ancient machinery.
- Recharge a Machine Core to power furnaces, blast furnaces and smokers.
- Hold a repairable Salvage Magnet to collect nearby dropped items.
- Choose Expedition, Balanced or Discoveries when creating a world. Discoveries
  is the factory preset for installations without a saved choice.

## Changes since Alpha 5.0.5

- Recognized incompatible saved-world environments now produce an explained
  rejection and return the client to the menu, preserving the frozen plan.
- Discoveries is the factory preset for new worlds without an existing saved
  installation choice. Existing choices and world plans retain their values.
- The displayed mod name is Titan Relics. Registry and save IDs remain `relics`.
- MIT and source-linked inspiration credits are included in all four JARs.
- Installation and player guides and a structured bug report form are available.

## Known limits and feedback

Back up saves. Start a new world with structures enabled for the complete relic
selection. Existing worlds keep their saved generation plans, including for
unexplored chunks. Updates do not move existing relics or refill finite shared
loot. Cross-loader save conversion and downgrading from 26.2 to 1.21.1 are not
supported. The 1.21.1 builds use ferns and ordinary chests instead of blocks
unavailable in that version.

Spacing and minimum-distance settings do not guarantee a nearby discovery.
There is one unique large Colossus destination per supported world.
DH 3.3.3 FEATURES can omit relics in distant generation. INTERNAL_SERVER passed
for newly generated distant areas. Old LOD-cache repair, large Voxy pregeneration
settings and long-term FPS/memory behavior remain unverified.
See [compatibility notes](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/blob/main/docs/technical/MOD_COMPATIBILITY.md).

The full natural Survival playthrough has not yet been completed. Report balance,
progression and technical issues through the
[bug report form](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml),
including exact versions, logs and reproduction steps. The
[player guide](https://github.com/Auda29/Titan-Relics/blob/main/docs/players/PLAYER_GUIDE.md)
explains the systems without listing every discovery.
