Important note for test worlds

Creative mode intentionally disables most interactive systems

If you are trying out Titan Relics in a Creative test world, switch to Survival (/gamemode survival) or Adventure before testing encounters or items. The following systems intentionally ignore Creative and Spectator players:

  • Great Colossus Trial Spawner: will not detect Creative players or spawn the guardian Iron Golem.
  • Great Colossus Vault: stays inactive, hides its preview item, and rejects Colossus Keys.
  • Personal reward drops: Colossus Keys and Machine Cores can only be picked up by their eligible owner in Survival/Adventure.
  • Salvage Magnet: will not attract dropped items in Creative or Spectator.
  • World Tree Aura & map compensation: do not apply effects or redeem emeralds in Creative or Spectator.

I / Suspicious Blocks & Excavation

Archaeology generates
only at Obelisks.

Every Obelisk has exactly 8 Suspicious Sand blocks (minecraft:suspicious_sand) placed around its ruins. Brush them rather than breaking them — breaking destroys the find permanently.

Find typeCount per ObeliskReward & odds
Expedition Map 1× guaranteed Expedition Map — Colossus pointing to your world’s unique Great Colossus (or 3 Emeralds directly if no valid Colossus site exists in that world).
Armor Trim 1× guaranteed Monolith Armor Trim Smithing Template.
Ordinary finds 6 blocks Rolled per block: 25% Pottery Sherd (equal chance of Angler, Archer, Arms Up, or Blade), 20% Clay Ball, 15% Brick, 10% Bone, 10% String, 10% Wheat Seeds, 5% Iron Nugget, 5% Copper Ingot.

Archaeology blocks are shared, finite finds per Obelisk. Once brushed or broken, they never refill on reload or for later players.

II / The Unique Great Colossus

How the Trial
& Vault work.

Every supported Overworld contains one unique Great Colossus. All Expedition Maps in that world lead to the same destination. The encounter chamber sits high inside the upper structure, entered through the upper hatch.

STEP 1 · TRIAL SPAWNER

The Guardian Iron Golem

  • Survival/Adventure required: Approach within 14 blocks of the Trial Spawner with clear line of sight.
  • No Ominous mode: Bad Omen / Trial Omen will not turn this spawner into an Ominous Trial.
  • Activation: After a 2-second (40-tick) wind-up, 1 persistent guardian Iron Golem spawns.
  • Multiplayer participation: Any eligible Survival/Adventure player within 14 blocks at activation — or who enters within 14 blocks while the Golem is alive — counts as a participant.
  • Reward & cooldown: Defeating the Golem drops 1 personal Colossus Key per participant (invulnerable, never despawns, owner-only Survival pickup), followed by a 30-minute cooldown (36,000 ticks). Non-combat removal aborts with no keys.
STEP 2 · VAULT

The Machine Core

  • Survival/Adventure required: Right-click the Vault beside the spawner while holding a Colossus Key.
  • Colossus Keys only: Vanilla Trial Keys are rejected. Colossus Keys can be traded or given to other players.
  • Once per player: Each player can permanently claim the Vault of a Great Colossus once.
  • Payout: Right-clicking consumes 1 Colossus Key immediately, plays a ~2.7-second animation, and ejects a full Machine Core (256 / 256) as a personal drop.
III / Every Exclusive Item

Acquisition, usage
& crafting recipes.

All five exclusive items in Titan Relics with exact drop locations, capacities, and crafting grids. Maintenance recipes unlock in your Recipe Book automatically once you hold the item.

ITEM 01 · NAVIGATION

Expedition Map — Colossus

How to obtain: 1× guaranteed in Suspicious Sand at every Obelisk.

How it works: A scale-2 map marked with an X pointing to your world’s unique Great Colossus; copyable like normal maps. While the background site search is still running it reads Unconfirmed and resolves automatically. If no valid Colossus site exists in the world (Destination Unavailable), right-click it in Survival/Adventure with free inventory space to redeem 3 Emeralds once for that origin.

ITEM 02 · ARMOR TRIM

Monolith Armor Trim Smithing Template

How to obtain: 1× guaranteed in Suspicious Sand at every Obelisk.

How it works: Apply the Monolith pattern to any trimmable armor at a Smithing Table using standard trim materials. Duplicate it in a Crafting Table to produce 2 templates.

ITEM 03 · KEY

Colossus Key

How to obtain: Trigger the Great Colossus Trial Spawner in Survival/Adventure and defeat its guardian Iron Golem.

How it works: Unlocks the Vault inside the Great Colossus in Survival/Adventure to award a Machine Core. Stackable and transferable between players.

ITEM 04 · FUEL SOURCE

Machine Core

How to obtain: Unlock the Great Colossus Vault with a Colossus Key in Survival/Adventure (once per player).

How it works: Place in the fuel slot of a Furnace, Blast Furnace, or Smoker. Keeps normal processing speed, holds 256 completed operations, and never drains while idle. Any regular fuel already burning finishes first. Hoppers can insert charged Cores and extract depleted ones (0 / 256) from below. Depleted Cores remain intact.

Recharge: Requires a fully depleted Core (0 / 256). Costs 1 Amethyst Shard + 2 Coal Blocks.

ITEM 05 · UTILITY

Salvage Magnet

How to obtain: Guaranteed full (1,000 / 1,000) inside the chest in the detached arm of the Great Colossus. Also a 10% chance to appear broken (0 / 1,000, needs repair) inside a Small Colossus chest.

How it works: Hold in your selected main hand or offhand while in Survival/Adventure (inactive in regular inventory slots). Pulls dropped items within 8 blocks with clear line of sight (solid blocks, glass, and closed doors/trapdoors block it) and available inventory space. Wear counts individual collected items (1,000 total); broken magnets remain intact at 0 / 1,000.

Repair: Works on any worn or broken Magnet (0–999 / 1,000). Costs 1 Amethyst Shard + 4 Iron Ingots.

V / World Settings & Versions

Presets, existing worlds
& 1.21.1 notes.

Before creating a world, open Create World → World → Relic generation to pick a play style.

PRESETS

Three play styles

  • Discoveries (default): 2,000-block spacing · 800-block min relic distance · Great Colossus from 4,000 blocks.
  • Balanced: 3,000-block spacing · 1,200-block min relic distance · Great Colossus from 8,000 blocks.
  • Expedition: 5,000-block spacing · 2,000-block min relic distance · Great Colossus from 15,000 blocks.

All presets use Matching landscapes (including classified modpack biomes). Minimum distances are lower bounds — terrain and biome checks can push valid sites farther away.

EXISTING WORLDS

Frozen rules

Each world freezes its generation settings and interior revision when created, even for unexplored chunks. Updates do not move relics, retrogen the Beta 4 upper tower chest into older worlds, or refill shared chests and archaeology.

MINECRAFT 1.21.1

Block differences

The 1.21.1 builds use ordinary Chests instead of Waxed Copper Chests and Ferns where newer 26.2+ blocks are unavailable. All gameplay mechanics, drops, recipes, and auras work identically.
