# Beta 3 screenshot sources

Seven screenshots supplied by the author from the 10 November 2026 playtest.
Minecraft Java 1.21.1, Titan Relics 1.0.0-beta.3, TerraVista Living World and
shaders. The modpack supplies additional terrain, vegetation and lighting.

The originals are 1920 × 1054, an exact ratio of 960:527. Each image is cropped
proportionally in both dimensions, then resampled to 960 × 527 for this repository
and CurseForge. Modrinth receives 1920 × 1054 exports of the same crop.
No image content was generated, inpainted, retouched or color-graded.
The original screenshots remain unchanged in the author's private archive.

is below 2 MB. The project gallery has not been changed through a file-upload API.

## Corrected subject labels

The author corrected these identifications on 10 November 2026. The retained
filenames do not all match their subjects: `sword-skull` shows Titan Skull,
`titan-skull` shows Small Colossus, `small-colossus` shows the great Colossus
from the front, and `colossus` shows the great Colossus from behind. No Sword
Skull photo is present in this set. Source pixels, hashes and filenames are
unchanged; use the corrected captions rather than inferring subjects from paths.
